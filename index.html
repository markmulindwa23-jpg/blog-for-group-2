My Story

My name is Kayanja Solomon, a student at Makerere University pursuing a Bachelor of Information Systems and Technology.

I am curious, ambitious and committed to continuous learning. I enjoy exploring technology, discovering new ideas, solving problems and understanding how digital solutions can improve people's lives.

Beyond academics, my Christian faith plays an important role in shaping my values, perspective and approach to life. This blog is a reflection of what I am learning, what I believe in and the person I am becoming.

What I Value

I believe education is more than earning qualifications. It is about developing the knowledge, skills and mindset needed to make a meaningful contribution to society.
